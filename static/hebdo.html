    Output

    Excel fills the hebdo_S38 template (Tickets, MTTR P1, MTTR P2). Ouvert counts open tickets of the status column (SLA = En cours in brise, blank Statut SLA Retablissement in GNOC); MTTR = Σ Duree ÷ count over the resolved tickets with Duree > 0 (blank/invalid durations excluded, warned) shown as HH:MM — with only one closed ticket in a domain, its Duree is the MTTR (formats: 6, 6,5, 36:30, 3:26:0, 5h30); cells are flagged against the SLA targets — P1 > 4 h / P2 > 8 h is BAD (red). A calculated 0 shows as 0 / 0.00%, no data shows as -.
